ESUC to ESA Rebranding

After many years of contemplation, Engineering Society at UCLA (ESUC) has changed our name officially to Engineering Student Association (ESA) at UCLA! Our decision to rebrand was a calculated decision with several underlying factors including new campus policies that restrict the use of UCLA in an organization name. The mission of our Engineering Student Association has not changed. As students from across the UCLA Samueli School of Engineering and Applied Science, we come together to serve our school, our students, staff, faculty, and alumni by hosting such school-wide events as Engineering Welcome Day and Bruin Engineers’ Week, representing our over 140 student organizations, design teams, and honor societies, and by advocating for fellow students. We are excited to continue to build up our Bruin Engineering community as the Engineering Student Association, to continue to support our engineering students, to build-upon and honor our 80+ years of service. For any questions, please contact Justin Xu, ESA President, at esa.ucla.president@gmail.com. Please stay patient as we continue to update everything. We thank you for your patience while we are rebranding and appreciate all your support.

Apply to be an ESA Active Member

Not wanting the time commitment of helping to plan ESA events? You can still participate in ESA events by helping volunteer, or just attending as an ESA active member! As a member, you'll get notified of all ESA events while not having to worry about the responsibilities of an intern or board member. Members may also use Engineering Passport check-ins at designated ESA events to record their participation and earn points toward end-of-year recognition. Passport points recognize involvement but do not affect your membership status.
